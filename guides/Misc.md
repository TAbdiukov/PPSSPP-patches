## Fired Up (PSP) - Undocumented Cheat

I found an **undocumented cheat** in *Fired Up*.

Set your player name to either:
* `QQQQQQQQ` (uppercase letter `Q x 8`)
* `HHHHHHHHH` (uppercase letter `H x 9`)

it enables godmode, infinite ammo, and unlocks the entire story.

Technical explanation: to enable godmode, the sum of ASCII characters in the name in the name must be equal 648.

Pretty wild that this was just sitting in the game undocumented.
